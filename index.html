Dear donor:

I want to tell you about Centro Deportivo Esparza, but before I share the project details...
I'll tell you how your donation, no matter how small, will help these children be happier and healthier.

These boys and girls are students at Colegio Esparza (most are between 4 and 17 years old), but even for something as fundamental as sports, they don't live in the conditions of the children you know.

The children you know, when they play soccer, probably do it on well-maintained grass fields, with safe goalposts, new balls, and specialized coaches.

They have scheduled times that are respected, sports hydration, locker rooms, first aid, and, above all, a space designed to protect their bodies and boost their development.

For those children, sports isn't a luxury. It's a natural part of growing up, and that's how it should be, because sports are a fundamental part of physical and mental well-being.

For the children at Colegio Esparza, the story is usually different.

Here, many children play soccer on hard concrete patios with mats that simulate grass, in improvised spaces that are often used as parking lots, without the basic infrastructure to train. They play without real goalposts, using tires and pillars, with bottles instead of balls or worn-out balls, under the intense sun and without a suitable place to hydrate or rest.

It's not that they don't want something better; in their world it doesn't exist or it's too expensive. For them, sports is a luxury they long for to take care of themselves and have fun.

And even so, they play.

They play with the passion of sweating and working hard with their classmates. They run, dream, compete, and push themselves like any child in the world. The difference isn't them. It's the conditions we give them.

Centro Deportivo Esparza is born to close that gap. So that these more than 300 boys and girls at the school can play sports in a dignified, safe, and formative environment. So that soccer and physical activity aren't just a momentary escape, but a real tool for health, discipline, teamwork, and hope.
